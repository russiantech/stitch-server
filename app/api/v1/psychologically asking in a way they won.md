psychologically asking in a way they won't refuse

1. Give a small gift first, then make your request, they'll feel they owe you and sould reciprocate by doing what you asked them to.
   
2. Don't ask directly, tell them they're the best at it and no one else could do it better, they'll feel special and unique and want to attend to it to prov they're as expert as you claimed, so they'll naturally say yes to protect the identity you gave them.
   
3. Because justification. Add the word BECAUSE + any reason to your request, even if the reason is weak, compliance increases dramatically. Example, Can you help me move on saturday, BECAUSE I really need a pair of hands.
